Privacy policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026. This page describes how Brew & Bloom (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our website.

Who we are

Brew & Bloom operates this website to share our menu, story, gallery, and reservation details. You can reach us at hello@brewandbloom.com or by phone at +91 98765 43210.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may process the following types of information:

  • Reservation or contact details: If you use our reservation or contact flows and choose to submit your name, phone number, email, date, party size, or similar fields, we use that information only to handle your request (for example, to confirm a table). This demo site may not send data to a live server; if we connect a backend later, we will process submissions according to this policy and any notice shown on the form.
  • Theme preference: We may store your light or dark theme choice in your browser (for example, localStorage) so the site remembers your preference. This stays on your device and is not used to identify you across other websites.
  • Technical data: Like most websites, hosting or infrastructure providers may automatically log basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, or approximate region) when you load pages. We use this only to operate and secure the site.

How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Respond to reservations, questions, or requests you send us;

We do not sell your personal information.

We keep optional preferences (such as theme) in browser storage where applicable. Embedded content (for example, a map on the visit page) may set or read cookies according to that provider’s own policy.

Retention

We keep form submissions and contact information only as long as needed to fulfill your request and for ordinary business or legal record-keeping, unless a shorter period is required by law.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of your personal data, or to object to processing. To exercise these rights or ask a question, contact us at the email or phone above.
