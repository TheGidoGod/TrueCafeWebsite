Our story

A small room, big comfort

Brew & Bloom started as a tiny espresso bar with two stools and one very loud grinder. Today we’re still obsessed with the details: dialed-in shots, bright teas, and pastry that tastes like it was made for your walk here.

  • Plant-forward options, always.
  • Gluten-free treats available daily.
  • Free Wi‑Fi, plenty of outlets, no rush.
